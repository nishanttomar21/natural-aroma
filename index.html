Bulk orders, checked before they leave our plant

What batch sizes do you produce at your distillation plant in Delhi NCR?

We run bulk production from our plant in Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR, for essential oils, spice oils, and aroma chemicals. Our lines handle regular repeat tonnage for formulators and manufacturers. Tell us your volume and we'll confirm we can meet it on schedule.

How does an ISO certified process keep my bulk order consistent?

We follow a documented ISO 9001:2008 certified process for every batch. That system catches variations before dispatch, unlike ad-hoc production. You get the same spec on a repeat order as you did on the first, which is what matters when you're buying in bulk.

Will you send me a sample before I commit to a full bulk order?

Yes. We send pre-production samples so you can verify the extract against your spec. Once you approve and the lot is distilled, we retain a batch sample for reference. It keeps both sides honest when a full order arrives.

What quality checks happen between distillation and dispatch?

Every batch is checked for key parameters like purity and odor profile before we approve it for dispatch, under our ISO 9001:2008 system. If a batch doesn't meet the agreed spec, it doesn't ship. That's the final gate before drums head to your facility.

Can your documentation fulfill compliance requirements for my business?

Yes. Each bulk order ships with documentation that records our packing and quality control steps. We run our processes to a certified standard, so you can trace the batch back to specific checks if you need to show your own auditors.
