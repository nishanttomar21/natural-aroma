Purity questions, answered from the plant floor

How do you ensure purity and sourcing of essential oils?

We source botanicals directly and distill them in our Ghaziabad plant using our modern distillation setup. Each batch is handled under our ISO 9001:2008 certified process, so you get a consistent product from our plant to your line.

Where are your raw materials sourced from?

We source from established growing regions, relying on decades of expertise since 1995 to select quality botanicals. When you place an order, we work to source the extract that fits your spec.

Can you provide documentation on product purity?

Yes, we provide specifications and quality documentation along with bulk orders. Since our process is ISO 9001:2008 certified, you can expect clear records on each batch we supply.

How do you keep batches consistent across bulk orders?

Our modern distillation plant is set up for bulk production, and we run a controlled process from intake to finished oil. That means you get the same profile, batch after batch.

Do you test for purity or contamination?

Every batch is produced under our ISO 9001:2008 certified quality system. We keep a close watch on the distillation process to maintain purity, and we can discuss testing details when you request a quote.

Are your oils and chemicals pure or blended?

We produce pure essential oils, spice oils, and aroma chemicals. From our plant in Delhi NCR, we supply what fits your production spec, whether that is a straight extract or a compound you need.
